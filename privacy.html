Privacy Policy Last updated: September 24, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Migratioon P handles information when you authorize the application to access your Pinterest account. 1. Purpose of the application Migratioon P is a personal utility created to assist with the migration and organization of Pinterest boards and Pins between Pinterest accounts owned and managed by the same person. The application is not intended to provide services to third parties or to access Pinterest accounts without the account owner's authorization. 2. Information accessed When authorized by the user through Pinterest's OAuth authorization process, the application may access information necessary to perform the requested migration, including Pinterest boards and Pins and information associated with those Pins and boards. The application only requests the permissions necessary for its intended functionality. 3. How information is used Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely to: Read the user's Pinterest boards and Pins. Create or organize boards and Pins in another Pinterest account owned and authorized by the same user. Perform the migration requested by the user. The information is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes. 4. Sharing of information The application does not sell, rent, or share the user's Pinterest information with third parties for their own purposes. Pinterest information accessed through the application is used only for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy. 5. Authentication and security The application uses Pinterest's OAuth authorization process. The application does not request or collect the user's Pinterest password. Access tokens and other authentication credentials, if stored, are handled securely and are not intentionally exposed publicly. 6. Data retention Pinterest information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to perform the requested migration or maintain the functionality of the application. The user may stop the application's access to their Pinterest account through Pinterest's account and application authorization controls. 7. Third-party services The application uses the Pinterest API to provide its functionality. Pinterest's own privacy practices and terms also apply to information processed by Pinterest. 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application or its privacy practices. The updated version will be published on this page. 9. Contact For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the Pinterest Business account used to administer the application.
